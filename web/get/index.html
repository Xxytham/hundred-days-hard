100 Days Hard

A free tracker for a 100-day gym challenge. No account needed.

Log food totals, daily tasks, Push / Pull / Legs workouts and body weight, track your strength like stocks, and climb your own goal ladders.

Android

Download for Android
  1. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  2. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from your browser.
  3. If Play Protect warns that it doesn't know the app, tap Install anyway. It isn't from the Play Store, which is why.

The app tells you when a new version is ready.

iPhone

Open the app
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open the app, then the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

It works offline and updates by itself. Your data stays on your phone, so save a backup file from the app now and then.
